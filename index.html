Good questions

Ask us anything

We're a new breed of dog care, so naturally there are questions. Here are the big ones.

See all FAQs
What does my dog need before their first visit?

A profile in the app, current rabies, distemper, parvo and Bordetella vaccines, and signed agreements. Daycare dogs also pass a short temperament screening first.

How are playgroups sorted?

By size, age and play style, based on the temperament screening. Dogs who prefer a quieter day can book a Day Stay instead of group play.

Is someone there overnight?

Yes. Our lodging is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and every supervisor is certified in pet first aid and CPR.

Can I see my dog while I'm away?

Luxury suites include a live webcam, and our team is happy to send updates during business hours.

Why do I book in the Paws & Pints app?

Mo'Tail & Spaw is part of the Paws & Pints family, so both share one app and one login. Read our story.

What if I need to cancel?

Lodging needs 48 hours' notice. Paws Pickup can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the trip.
