Four steps

From download to day one

  1. Get the app

    Download the app and create your account. It's shared with our sister brand, Paws & Pints, so one login covers both.

  2. Add your dog

    Build their profile: name, breed, age, and anything we should know, from allergies to favorite games.

  3. Upload vaccines & sign

    Add their vaccine records and sign the agreements under the Agreements tab.

  4. Book day one

    Pick a service and a date. For daycare, day one starts with a quick temperament screening.

Get ready

The checklist

Required vaccines

  • Rabies
  • Distemper
  • Parvo
  • Bordetellakennel cough

Upload them to your dog's profile in the app before the first visit.

Your dog should be

  • In good health, free of fleas and ticks
  • Free of open wounds or stitches
  • Not pregnant or in heat
  • Spayed or neutered for group play if over 6 months (Day Stays welcome everyone)

Bring for a stay

  • Pre-bagged food, labeled with their name and AM/PM
  • Medication in the original bottle
  • A washable blanket from home (optional)
  • Up to 2 toys, no squeakers

On day one

  • Come to the front desk with your dog on a leash
  • Use a quick-snap collar for easy on and off
  • Daycare dogs start with a temperament screening
  • Then the fun starts

Good questions

First-timer FAQ

More answers on our full FAQ, or call (515) 969-2275.

Is there a minimum age?

[Owner to answer] The youngest age you accept for daycare, lodging and grooming.

My dog isn't spayed or neutered. Can they still come?

Yes. Dogs over six months who aren't fixed can't join group play, but they're welcome for Day Stays, lodging and grooming.

Do you have breed restrictions?

Never. Every dog is welcome to try, and every dog passes the same temperament screening.

What happens at the temperament screening?

[Owner to answer] How long it takes, what you'll see, and whether you stay for it.

What if my dog's vaccines have lapsed?

They'll need to be current before their first visit. Upload the updated records in the app once your vet has them.

Can I just drop in?

We work by booking, so reserve in the app or call first. Tours run on the hour, 11 AM–3 PM, on weekdays.
